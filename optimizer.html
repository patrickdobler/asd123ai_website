Processing Options

Apply Language Character Mapping
Replaces special characters like quotes, dashes, and spaces according to the selected language mapping. Useful for normalizing text from different sources.
Remove Diacritics
Converts accented characters to their basic equivalents (e.g., è → e, ä → ae, ñ → n). Useful for Swiss German and international text compatibility.
Remove Citation References
Removes source and citation references commonly found in AI outputs, such as [1], [2], (Source: XYZ), etc. Perfect for cleaning up Perplexity or research-based AI responses.
Convert MD to Normal Text
Converts Markdown formatting to plain text by removing **bold**, *italic*, # headers, and other Markdown syntax commonly used in AI responses.
Remove Fancy Text Font
Converts fancy Unicode text styles (𝐛𝐨𝐥𝐝, 𝑖𝑡𝑎𝑙𝑖𝑐, 𝖒𝖔𝖓𝖔𝖘𝖕𝖆𝖈𝖊, etc.) back to plain characters. Useful for normalizing text copied from social media or formatted documents.
Replace em dash with new sentence EXPERIMENTAL
Experimental: Replaces em dashes ("—") with a full stop and starts a new sentence. This feature attempts to extract coherent sentences from em-dash-separated clauses commonly found in AI outputs.
