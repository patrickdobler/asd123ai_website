AI Text Processing

Tools to clean, anonymize, estimate context, and chat with AI locally in your browser. Built for privacy, speed, and reliability.

Optimizer

Effortlessly clean up and standardize AI-generated text by replacing special characters, fixing encoding issues, and ensuring smooth integration for any region or workflow. Perfect for Swiss users, ready for the world.

Anonymizer

Explore local PII detection, anonymization, and deanonymization workflows directly in your browser. This tool is an experimental proof of concept and should be reviewed carefully before use with sensitive material.

Chat

Run a private WebGPU AI chat directly in your browser. Choose local models, attach documents or images, edit prompts, export Markdown, and keep chat history on your device.

Context

Estimate how much AI chat context your text or document needs before choosing 4K, 8K, 16K, or larger local model settings. TXT, DOCX, and PDF files are processed in your browser.

Converter

Turn PDF, DOCX, PPTX, Excel, CSV, and HTML documents into clean Markdown right in your browser. Headings, lists, links, and tables are preserved without ever uploading the file.

Speech

Turn text into natural speech locally with on-device models like Kokoro and Supertonic, in English, German, French, Spanish, and Italian. Pick a voice, adjust speed, then play or download the audio as WAV or MP3. Your text never leaves your device.
