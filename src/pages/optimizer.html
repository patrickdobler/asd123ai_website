Processing Options

A preset switches the toggles below to a proven combination — fine-tune afterwards if needed.
Flattens quotes, dashes, ellipses and PDF ligatures to plain ASCII. Only the language-specific rules differ — Swiss German turns ß into ss, French drops the space inside « guillemets », Universal never touches letters.
Character Processing
Remove Invisible Characters
Strips zero-width characters, bidi controls, variation selectors, tag characters and other hidden formatting, and folds unusual spaces back to a normal space. Emoji stay intact. After cleaning you get a breakdown of what was found.
Covers 441 invisible code points — hidden tracking marks and AI watermarks, zero-width characters from copy-paste, and bidi controls that make text read differently than it is stored.
Apply Language Character Mapping
Replaces special characters like quotes, dashes, and spaces according to the selected language mapping
Remove Diacritics
Converts accented characters to plain equivalents. With German or Swiss German selected, ä/ö/ü become ae/oe/ue; for other languages they become a/o/u.
Remove Fancy Text Font
Converts all stylized Unicode alphabets (bold, italic, script, double-struck, fullwidth, ...) back to plain characters
Content Cleaning
Remove Citation References
Removes citation markers such as [1], superscript references, (Source: ...) notes and citation links. Normal links keep their visible text.
Convert MD to Normal Text
Converts Markdown formatting to plain text
Replace em dash with new sentence
Replaces em dashes with a full stop and starts a new sentence
Remove Line Breaks
Removes all line breaks and joins text into a single continuous paragraph
