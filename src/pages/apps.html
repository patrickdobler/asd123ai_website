Apps

Open-source software from ASD123 - the same privacy-first mindset as the browser tools, beyond the browser. Free, open source, no telemetry.

RDP123

A minimal, fast RDP client for macOS. Native AppKit written in Rust - about 8 MB installed, lives in the menu bar, latency-obsessed rendering, passwords in the Keychain, no telemetry.

Photo-Shot

A mobile-first, self-hosted PWA for AI image editing across Gemini, WaveSpeed and OpenRouter (14+ models), plus an EXIF metadata copier. Your API keys stay on your own server.

RustQuit

Automatically quits macOS apps when their last window closes - the red ✕ finally means quit. A tiny, defensive menu bar utility in Rust: graceful ⌘Q only, protected system apps, no telemetry.

Why these apps exist

Like the browser tools on this site, these apps come from everyday needs with a clear privacy boundary: software should do its job on your own devices - no telemetry, no accounts, no data leaving your control. Every app here is free software (GPL or AGPL v3): the source is on GitHub, and you are welcome to read, build, and improve it.
