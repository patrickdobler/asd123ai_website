Processing Options

A preset switches the toggles below to a proven combination — fine-tune afterwards if needed.
Character Processing
Remove Invisible Characters & Watermarks
Covers 441 invisible code points: zero-width characters, bidi controls, variation selectors, tag characters and other hidden formatting, plus unusual spaces folded back to a normal space. This catches character-based watermarks and tracking marks - the invisible markers some tools embed in generated text. Statistical watermarks, woven into word choice itself, leave no character behind and are not affected. Emoji stay intact, and after cleaning you get a breakdown of what was found.
Apply Character Mapping
Flattens typography to plain ASCII: every dash and quotation mark, ellipses, bullets, primes and the ligatures that text extracted from PDFs is full of. Never touches letters on its own - the three switches below add that.
Hyphens & dashes
Every dash and the minus sign become the ASCII hyphen: ‐ ‑ ‒ – — ― −. Switch off to keep em dashes as they are. 7 rules.
Quotation marks
Double quotes flattened to the ASCII ": „“”, guillemets « », and the double prime. 8 rules.
Apostrophes
Single quotes, apostrophes, primes and the spacing accents used in their place, all flattened to the ASCII apostrophe. 10 rules.
Bullets, ellipses & symbols
Ellipsis and two-dot leader spelled out, bullet characters turned into a hyphen, and the multiplication sign and fraction slash replaced by x and /. 9 rules.
PDF ligatures
Text extracted from PDFs often stores fi and fl as a single glyph. This splits them back apart: ﬁnal becomes final. 7 rules.
Spell out æ/œ as ae/oe
German, French and English spell these out in ASCII. Italian has no æ/œ of its own, so leave this off to keep foreign words intact.
Convert ß to ss
Switzerland does not use the sharp s. Germany and Austria do, so leave this off for German from those countries.
French spacing rules
French typography puts a space inside « guillemets » and before ; : ! ? - this removes it, so « bonjour » becomes "bonjour".
Remove Diacritics
Converts accented characters to plain equivalents. With German or Swiss German selected, ä/ö/ü become ae/oe/ue; for other languages they become a/o/u.
Umlauts as ae/oe/ue
The German and Swiss convention: ä/ö/ü become ae/oe/ue. With this off they become the plain a/o/u.
Remove Fancy Text Font
Converts all stylized Unicode alphabets (bold, italic, script, double-struck, fullwidth, ...) back to plain characters
Content Cleaning
Remove Citation References
Removes citation markers such as [1], superscript references, (Source: ...) notes and citation links. Normal links keep their visible text.
Convert MD to Normal Text
Converts Markdown formatting to plain text
Replace em dash with new sentence
Replaces em dashes with a full stop and starts a new sentence
Remove Line Breaks
Removes all line breaks and joins text into a single continuous paragraph
