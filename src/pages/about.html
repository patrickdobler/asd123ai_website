About

Learn more about ASD123.ai and our mission to provide privacy-first AI text processing tools.

Made in Switzerland for Privacy

Your privacy is our core principle. All data is processed directly in your browser. No information ever leaves your device, ensuring complete confidentiality and security. This project is built on the foundation of Swiss privacy standards.

Why ASD123.ai exists

ASD123.ai is a small collection of practical browser tools for people who work with AI text but do not want their drafts, documents, prompts, or sensitive data to become part of another service. The goal is simple: useful AI workflows with clear privacy boundaries.

The tools focus on everyday friction points: cleaning copied or AI-generated text, estimating context before loading a local model, experimenting with local anonymization, and chatting with browser-based models where supported. The project is intentionally transparent, lightweight, and review-friendly. Results should still be checked by a human, especially when privacy, compliance, or business-critical work is involved.
