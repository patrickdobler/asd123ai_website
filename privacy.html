Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Learn how we protect your data in accordance with Swiss data protection laws.

Last updated: January 23, 2025

Effective date: January 23, 2025

1. Data Controller

This website is operated by Patrick Dobler. For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at contact@asd123.ai.

2. Privacy-First Architecture

ASD123.ai is built with privacy as a core principle. Our text processing tools operate entirely within your browser (client-side processing). This means:

  • Your text data never leaves your device
  • No text content is transmitted to our servers
  • All processing happens locally in your browser
  • We cannot access, view, or store your text data

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Technical Data

When you visit our website, we may collect standard technical information including:

  • IP address (anonymized)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring website
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Date and time of access

3.2 Local Storage

We use browser local storage to save your preferences (such as language mapping settings) locally on your device. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3.3 Contact Information

When you contact us via email, we collect and process the information you provide to respond to your inquiry.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under Swiss data protection law (nDSG) and GDPR, we process personal data based on:

  • Legitimate interest for technical data to ensure website functionality and security
  • Consent for local storage of preferences
  • Contract performance when responding to your inquiries

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • When required by Swiss or applicable law
  • To protect our rights or the safety of others
  • With service providers who assist in website operation (under strict confidentiality agreements)

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all communications
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Client-side processing to minimize data exposure

7. Your Rights

Under Swiss data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about data we process about you
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Restriction: Limit processing of your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@asd123.ai.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Technical logs are typically retained for 30 days for security purposes.

9. International Transfers

Our website is hosted on Cloudflare's global network. Data may be processed in countries outside Switzerland that provide adequate data protection or under appropriate safeguards.

10. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies and tracking technologies. Essential cookies are used for website functionality. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies without your consent.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on our website.

12. Contact Information

For questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact:

Patrick Dobler
Email: contact@asd123.ai

13. Supervisory Authority

If you have concerns about our data processing practices, you may contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) at www.edoeb.admin.ch.
